Your information

Privacy, explained clearly.

This policy explains what information I collect through edward-hallam.com, why I use it, where it is stored and the choices you have.

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Who is responsible

Edward Hallam is the data controller for this website and operates as a sole trader in England and Wales.

For privacy questions or requests, email edwardhallam07@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Enquiries: your name, phone number, message and consent choice.
  • Waiting lists: your email address, the list you joined and your consent choice.
  • Cookie choices: the preferences you save for necessary, preference, analytics and marketing technologies.
  • Technical information: hosting and security providers may process information such as your IP address, browser, device, requested page and request time to deliver and protect the site.

This information comes directly from you when you submit a form, or from the technical providers that deliver and secure the website.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in a general enquiry unless it is necessary and we have agreed an appropriate way to share it.

Why I use it

  • To answer a message, discuss requested work and take steps you ask for before entering a contract.
  • To manage an optional waiting-list request where you have given consent.
  • To maintain site security, prevent abuse and diagnose faults.
  • To meet legal, accounting or regulatory duties where they apply.
  • To measure site use or campaign performance only when you allow the relevant optional cookies.

The lawful bases used are steps at your request before a contract, legitimate interests in responding to genuine enquiries and protecting the site, legal obligation where records must be kept, and consent for waiting-list messages and optional cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.

I do not use this information to make solely automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

AquaCRM and suppliers

Form submissions are sent to AquaCRM, the system used to organise enquiries and follow-up. AquaCRM does not receive information from a form until you choose to submit it.

Trusted providers may process information on my behalf, including Vercel for website hosting, Supabase for database and storage services, and Postmark for transactional email delivery. They may only use information to provide their contracted service and protect their systems.

Some providers may process information outside the UK. Where this happens, I rely on the provider's appropriate legal safeguards for international transfers.

Cookies

Necessary storage is used to remember your privacy choices and support core operation. Optional preference, analytics and marketing technologies remain off unless you allow them.

Analytics is configured not to record the values you type into forms. Form consent is separate from cookie consent. Use the Cookie settings button on this page at any time to change your choices.

How long I keep it

General enquiries are normally kept for up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact, unless the conversation becomes a customer relationship or a longer period is needed for legal reasons.

Waiting-list information is kept until you unsubscribe, the list is closed, or it is no longer needed. Contract, payment and accounting records may be kept for longer where the law requires it. Information is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer required.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or receive a copy of your personal information. You may also object to certain uses and withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests and to direct marketing at any time.

Send requests to edwardhallam07@gmail.com. I may need to confirm your identity before completing a request.

Complaints and changes

Please contact me first so I can try to resolve a concern. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office through ico.org.uk.

This policy may be updated when the website, suppliers or legal obligations change. The latest version and update date will remain on this page.

Need something clarified or removed? Email edwardhallam07@gmail.com and tell me what you need.
